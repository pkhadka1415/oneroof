# 🏠 OneRoof

**A community platform for students and young people in the Bay Area.**

OneRoof brings housing, food, events, free resources, job referrals, and emotional support together in one place — so students don't have to juggle five different apps to find what they need.

## 🌐 Live Demo

**[Try OneRoof](https://YOUR_USERNAME.github.io/oneroof/)**

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Housing & Roommates** — post and browse rooms, subleases, and roommate listings
- 🍽️ **Community Posts** — share food meetups, events, and free items
- 💼 **Job Referrals** — connect with community members who can refer you
- 💬 **Hear Me Out** — a safe space to share what's on your mind with supportive reactions
- 🚩 **Reporting & Moderation** — keep the community safe with reporting tools and a moderator panel
- 🔍 **Search** — find listings and posts across all categories
- ✅ **Email Verification** — build trust with verified profiles

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript (vanilla, ES modules)
- **Backend:** Firebase (Authentication + Firestore)
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages
