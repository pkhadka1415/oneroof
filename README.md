**A community platform for students and young professionals in the Bay Area.**

OneRoof brings housing, food, events, free resources, job referrals, safety alerts, and emotional support together in one place — so you don't need five different apps to find what you need.

## 🌐 Live Demo

**[Try OneRoof](https://pkhadka1415.github.io/oneroof/)**

## ✨ Features

### 🏡 Housing
Find rooms, subleases, and roommates. Filter by type — landlord, sublease, rent room, shared single, shared apartment. Mark listings as "Interested" to show interest. Leave public comments and reply to others.

### 🍽️ Food
Connect over meals with five post types:
- 🍴 Dining Out — find company for a restaurant
- 💡 Recommendations — ask for restaurant suggestions
- 👨‍🍳 Cooking Partner — find someone to cook with
- 📖 Recipe Ideas — ask what to make with ingredients
- 🆘 Cooking Help — get cooking guidance

### 🎉 Events
Find friends through shared activities:
- 🎉 Going Out — concerts, bars, clubs
- 🥾 Explore Together — hikes, outdoor activities
- 💡 Recommendations — ask for fun ideas
- 👥 Activity Partner — gym buddies, tennis partners
- 🆓 Free Event — free things to do

### 🎁 Free & Help
Seven types of peer help:
- 🎁 Stuff — give away items
- 📦 Request — ask for items
- 🚗 Ride — offer or request rides
- 🔧 Lend — borrow or lend tools
- 🛠️ Skill — resume reviews, tutoring
- 📚 Study — study partners, homework help
- 💬 General — anything else

### 💼 Jobs
Job referrals across any industry — food service, cafés, hospitality, retail, tech, healthcare, trades, education, and more.

### 💬 Hear Me Out
A safe space to share what's on your mind. Post anonymously or with your name. Receive supportive reactions and comments. Moderated for safety.

### 🚨 Crime Alerts
Community safety reports — crimes, suspicious activity, unsafe areas. Mark yourself as "Aware" to stay informed.

### 📬 Messages
Private in-app messaging. No email exposure — communicate safely through OneRoof.

### 💎 Trust System
- **Vouches** — written endorsements from people you've interacted with
- **Diamond ratings** — 5-diamond scale attached to each vouch
- **Follows** — subscribe to users you trust
- **Verification** — email verification badge

### 🚩 Moderation
Report inappropriate content. Moderators review reports and resolve them. Community guidelines keep the space respectful.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript (ES modules)
- **Backend:** Firebase Authentication + Firestore
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages
- **Real-time:** Firestore listeners
- **Security:** Firestore rules with owner-based permissions
